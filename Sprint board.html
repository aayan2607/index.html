Product Goal

Help a small team see, order and finish its work on one simple board.

Scrum Team

  • Product Owner: you. Orders the Product Backlog and accepts finished work.
  • Scrum Master: keeps events time-boxed and clears blockers.
  • Developers: Claude. Builds a usable Increment each sprint.

Events

  • Sprint: one week, one Sprint Goal.
  • Sprint Planning: pick stories that fit last velocity.
  • Daily Scrum: 15 minutes. What moved, what is blocked.
  • Sprint Review: demo the Increment, adjust the backlog.
  • Sprint Retrospective: choose one improvement.
